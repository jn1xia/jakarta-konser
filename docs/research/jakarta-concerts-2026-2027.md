# Jakarta concerts, 11 Nov 2026 – 31 Dec 2027

Snapshot taken **11 November 2026**. It covers Jakarta and the Greater Jakarta venues fans
treat as "Jakarta" shows (ICE BSD in Tangerang).

**How this was gathered, and its limits.** Direct access to news, promoter and ticketing sites
is blocked in the build sandbox, so everything here comes from web-search summaries of those
sites. Dates and venues that several outlets agree on are marked **confirmed**; anything from one
outlet only is marked **single source**. Small club shows and Indonesian solo concerts are the
most likely to be missing. Nothing has been announced yet for May–December 2027 apart from the
unofficial Java Jazz dates, so this list needs a refresh every month or so.

## Left out on purpose

- Shows already past on 11 Nov 2026: Andrea Bocelli (3 Nov, JIExpo), King Nassar (7 Nov, Istora),
  Remember Fest (7–8 Nov), BABYMONSTER (17 Oct), iKON (21 Aug).
- "Skye Riley at BritAma Arena, 5–6 Apr 2027" on one aggregator: Skye Riley is a fictional pop
  star from the film *Smile 2*, so this is a junk listing.
- "Big Bang Festival" at JIExpo (25 Dec – 5 Jan) is a clearance-sale fair, not a concert.
